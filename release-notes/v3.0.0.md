## One Pace Downloader 3.0

The Windows app has been rebuilt from scratch as a native Windows 11 app.

**Fast.** Clicking arcs and episodes is instant, scrolling is smooth, and the app starts in a moment.

**Looks like Windows.** Mica background, your Windows accent colour, and light and dark themes that follow your system. It stays sharp at any display scaling.

**Everything you had, plus:**
- Settings is now a page, like Windows Settings, and changes apply right away.
- Toast notifications when downloads finish or magnets and NZBs are sent.
- A failed episode no longer stops a batch: the rest keep going and you get a list of what to retry.
- Shift+click to tick a range of episodes, Ctrl+A to tick all, Ctrl+F to search arcs, F5 to check for new episodes.
- Downloads switch to the faster mirror automatically when Pixeldrain slows down or hits its daily limit, resume where they stopped, and every file is checked against its CRC32.

Your settings and downloads carry over: keep the new exe in the same folder as the old one.

Every episode in the catalog is now checked automatically each week to make sure it still downloads.
